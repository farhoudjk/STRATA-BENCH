\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We compared \vllm, \sglang, and \trt under load across six user-facing metrics and SLO goodput,
three models with different KV-cache footprints, realistic workloads, and two hardware platforms.
The engines behave alike until a resource saturates; beyond that point they differ mainly in how
they admit requests and where they make them wait, and those differences decide which engine
meets a given SLO. No engine is best across workloads, metrics, and features: the fastest decoder
can deliver the lowest goodput, and a feature that helps one engine can hurt another. We hope the
decision guide and the methodology lessons help practitioners choose and evaluate serving engines
