\label{sec:engines}

\Cref{tab:engines} lists the engines and platforms. All engines serve fp16 weights on one GPU
(no tensor parallelism) with a maximum context of 8{,}192 tokens. The scheduler limit $S$ maps to
each engine's equivalent setting. \todo{list the per-engine flags: KV memory fraction, maximum

\subsection{Models}
\label{sec:models}

We choose three instruction-tuned models whose per-token KV footprint
$2 L H_{kv} d b$ (\cref{eq:kvcap}) spans a wide range (\cref{tab:models}).
Qwen1.5-14B-Chat~\cite{bai2023qwen} has no KV-head reduction ($H_{kv}=40$) and needs $5\times$ the
cache per token of Mistral-NeMo-12B and $6.25\times$ that of Llama-3.1-8B~\cite{dubey2024llama3}.
We considered distilled reasoning models but rejected them after confirming from their
configuration files that they use GQA backbones, which would not provide the contrast.

\subsection{Workloads}
\label{sec:workloads}

We instantiate three workloads from public corpora (\cref{tab:workloads}).
\texttt{mixed\_real} is a mixture (\cref{eq:workload}) of four tasks with $\pi_k = 1/4$: chat
(ShareGPT~\cite{sharegpt}), code generation (MBPP~\cite{austin2021mbpp}), reasoning
(GSM8K~\cite{cobbe2021gsm8k}), and summarisation (CNN/DailyMail~\cite{hermann2015cnn}). It pairs
short prompts with very long output budgets (reasoning) and long prompts with short outputs
(summarisation), so a few long-running requests can hold scheduler slots and KV memory for
minutes. \texttt{chat\_multiturn} replays ShareGPT conversations of two to six turns.
\texttt{chat\_real\_long} uses single-turn ShareGPT prompts of at least 500 tokens; prompts longer
than the context (1\%) are rejected by the client and excluded.

\subsection{Load and measurement parameters}
\label{sec:params}

Arrival processes are Poisson with $\lambda \in \{2, 4, 8, 16\}$ requests/s and bursty with
$B = 10$, $\delta = 50$\,ms, $T_B = 5$\,s. The client concurrency limit is $C \in \{16, 256\}$
and the scheduler limit $S \in \{64, 256\}$. Timeouts are $\tau_1 = 300$\,s and $\tau_2 = 600$\,s.
Engine metrics are polled every $\Delta t$ \verify{} seconds. We evaluate goodput (\cref{eq:goodput})
at a \emph{strict} target $(T^\star, P^\star) = (2\,\mathrm{s}, 200\,\mathrm{ms})$ and a
\emph{relaxed} target $(4\,\mathrm{s}, 500\,\mathrm{ms})$, representative of interactive chat.
